## 2. Frozen candidate set and fit assessment

### Candidate A — Chaos: An Interdisciplinary Journal of Nonlinear Science (AIP)

Fit: **STRONG / SELECTED PRIMARY**.

Strengths:

- explicit nonlinear-dynamics and complex-systems scope;
- explicitly interdisciplinary and application-friendly;
- current coverage includes nonlinear dynamics of computation and applications of nonlinear phenomena in other fields;
- current issues publish Regular Articles spanning theory, computation, learning, nonautonomous dynamics, control-adjacent and applied nonlinear systems;
- a Regular Article can preserve the full mandatory WEAK/FAIL/Gram/PARK and mean/COI evidence chain;
- AIP accepts the existing SVG figure format;
- supplementary material is supported as a separate reviewed PDF.

Risk:

- the manuscript must still establish a meaningful original research contribution despite its deliberately restricted novelty claims. The safe contribution remains Package P only: the synergetics-centered diagnostic organisation plus the prospectively frozen witness/countercontrol/preparation evidence chain.

### Candidate B — Physical Review E (APS)

Fit: **GOOD / FALLBACK ONLY**.

PRE explicitly covers statistical, nonlinear and complex systems, networks/interdisciplinary physics, computational physics, machine learning and AI. Regular Articles have no length limit and a Data Availability Statement is required.

Why not primary: PRE's current editorial criteria emphasise a high-quality, significant and substantive addition to physics. The frozen manuscript is intentionally a claim-restricted diagnostic synthesis whose contribution is organisational/evidential rather than a new physics criterion or new universal law. A PRE submission is defensible, but it would carry a higher editorial-fit risk without any permitted claim strengthening.

### Candidate C — Journal of Physics: Complexity (IOP)

Fit: **GOOD / NOT SELECTED**.

JPhys Complexity has broad interdisciplinary complex-systems scope and allows unlimited-length Research Papers. It also supports single- or double-anonymous review by author choice.

Why not primary: its current guidance emphasises significant/original science and describes research articles as reporting breakthrough results of significant importance. The frozen manuscript cannot and must not inflate individual novelty to meet such language. It is also fully open access with a current APC of GBP 1985 / EUR 2355 / USD 2730 before applicable discounts/agreements. These points do not make it unsuitable, but Chaos is the cleaner claim-compatible fit.

## 4. Frozen Chaos submission-format specification

### 4.1 Article type

Use **Regular Article / Research Article**.

Do **not** use Chaos Fast Track. Current Chaos Fast Track guidance imposes a 5750-word equivalent limit and is intended for a compressed accelerated format. Compressing the frozen manuscript to that format would create unnecessary pressure to remove mandatory negative evidence, successful countercontrols, prior-art boundaries or limitation language.

The current Chaos-specific author instructions state a length limit for Fast Track but do not state a separate hard word/page cap for Regular Articles. Therefore no unverified hard Regular-Article limit is invented here. The Regular Article should remain concise but may preserve the full frozen evidence chain.

### 4.2 Initial submission files

Current AIP general instructions require:

- one compiled manuscript PDF for initial submission;
- supplementary material, if used, as a separate supplementary-material PDF;
- Word or LaTeX source is acceptable; AIP recommends its LaTeX template/Overleaf route for LaTeX users.

Frozen implementation choice for the next adaptation task:

- use the AIP LaTeX template for the venue-specific source;
- do not modify the canonical editorial Markdown source;
- create a new versioned Chaos-specific directory only.

### 4.3 Abstract

AIP requires a one-paragraph abstract of no more than 250 words, without displayed equations, references, footnotes, graphics or tables.

The frozen editorial abstract is approximately 274 words and therefore requires a **claim-neutral shortening only**.

The adapted abstract must retain the frozen four-element architecture:

1. passive/current macro sufficiency versus declared intervention sufficiency;
2. standard controlled-projectability/closure bridge, explicitly not claimed as new;
3. frozen neural and power-grid witnesses/countercontrols plus preparation benchmark;
4. explicit no-new-quotient / no-universal-law / no-generic-control limitation.

No strengthening is permitted while shortening.

### 4.4 Chaos Lead Paragraph

Chaos requires the first article paragraph to serve as a Lead Paragraph giving the main points and big picture for non-specialist readers.

The next adaptation may rewrite/reorder the first Introduction paragraph only to satisfy that role, provided:

- the central diagnostic question remains unchanged;
- Package P remains the sole contribution-bearing framing;
- standard/projectability prior art is acknowledged immediately;
- the neural WEAK/FAIL/Gram/PARK and power-grid mean/COI limitations are not hidden;
- no novelty/priority wording is introduced.

Background displaced from the first paragraph may be moved to later Introduction/Related Work paragraphs without scientific change.

### 4.5 Figures and tables

AIP accepts SVG, EPS, PS, TIFF, PDF, JPEG and PNG graphics. The four frozen SVG figures therefore remain format-compatible.

Rules:

- keep the four scientific figure artifacts unchanged in content;
- embed figures in the manuscript at appropriate locations;
- retain their figure numbers and scientific roles;
- no trajectory regeneration or quantitative redrawing;
- purely typographic scaling/layout changes are permitted only if labels/content remain unchanged;
- provide concise alt text for every figure; alt text must describe the scientific information neutrally and must not add conclusions or claims.

If no separate highlight image is later supplied, AIP may use Figure 1; no new highlight artwork is authorised by this gate.

### 4.6 Supplementary material

The complete frozen Appendices A–F remain supplementary material.

Venue adaptation may:

- convert the byte-identical scientific/textual supplement substance into AIP-compatible typesetting;
- add a brief `Supplementary Material` notice after the main Conclusion as required by AIP;
- upload/render the supplement separately downstream.

It may not remove, rewrite upward, or reinterpret the WEAK/FAIL/classifier/Gram/PARK material or the exact mean/COI control.

### 4.7 References

AIP general instructions accept numerical, alphabetical author-year, or numbered-alphabetical reference formats.

Frozen adaptation choice:

- retain the existing author-year citation logic to minimise content churn;
- normalise it to a consistent AIP-accepted alphabetical author-year style;
- preserve the exact cited-work set and mandatory citation placement from `RP-021`/`RP-020`;
- retain article titles where already available;
- no new literature search and no new reference may be added during formatting without MASTER authorisation.

### 4.8 Required declaration/availability sections

AIP requires or expects the venue-specific manuscript to contain the applicable declaration block, including:

- Conflict of Interest statement, including an explicit no-conflict statement if applicable;
- CRediT Author Contributions statement;
- Data Availability Statement;
- acknowledgments/funding information as applicable;
- author/affiliation/corresponding-author information.

Administrative author/funder/COI details are not yet scientific content. If unavailable in Git, the MANUSCRIPT adaptation may mark them as explicit `AUTHOR INPUT REQUIRED` placeholders; it must not invent them.

For the Data Availability Statement, the public repository `twkroll/causal_synergetics` and the frozen reproducibility manifest may be cited as the source of code/result pointers. The wording must not claim external reproducibility or CI success beyond the frozen manifest.

### 4.9 Review/anonymisation

No double-anonymous manuscript preparation requirement was identified for Chaos in the current AIP author instructions. AIP preserves reviewer anonymity unless a reviewer voluntarily signs the report. The venue-specific manuscript should therefore retain named author metadata once supplied rather than being anonymised by default.

### 4.10 Submission system metadata

AIP's current submission workflow requires online entry/upload of manuscript information including title/abstract, author/contact information, declarations and files; the system may also request reviewer suggestions/exclusions and publication/funding information.

This gate does not create an account, populate Peer X-Press, contact editors, choose reviewers, or submit anything.

### 4.11 Access model / fees

Chaos is an AIP subscription journal with optional Author Select open access. AIP currently lists Author Select at USD 3800 for subscription-journal articles.

No open-access payment decision is made here. The default packaging assumption is the standard subscription route unless an author/funder requirement later mandates or motivates Author Select. Any payment or OA-license choice requires a later explicit administrative decision.
