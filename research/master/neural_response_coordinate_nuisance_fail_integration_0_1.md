### Direct frozen fact

The finite evaluation design contains four gauge-equivalent copies of each response-latent state per evaluation partition. A one-state lexicographic cyclic shift can therefore map many states to another gauge copy with the same response coordinate and same true response. This explains why N0 can retain substantial predictive score.

## 8. Reasons for rejecting alternatives

### GO to broader nonlinear/multi-step work — rejected

The current coordinate evidence does not justify broader scaling. The original held-out coordinate pilot was WEAK versus equal-dimensional raw PCA, and the nuisance control shows that the apparent advantage over naive raw coordinates can be matched exactly by a known symmetry-aware raw-state quotient.

### REVISE with orbit-level/null-method validation — rejected

A prospective orbit-level or latent-derangement null could make the scientific classifier total and better test state association, but its design would now be explicitly motivated by the observed N0 failure. More importantly, even a successful repaired validation would not change the dominant scientific ceiling: B3 already matches the response coordinate exactly. The incremental information value is therefore too low relative to the risk of iterative post-hoc benchmark repair.

### Alternate nuisance/family/coordinate — rejected

That would amount to effect-guided search after both a WEAK coordinate result and a specification FAIL, violating the anti-cherry-picking discipline of the programme.

### Stop the entire programme — rejected

The broader programme still retains frozen CORE results, exact intervention-insufficiency witnesses, the neural linear/history/ReLU feasibility chain, and open second-domain/application questions. Parking one coordinate direction does not invalidate those results.
