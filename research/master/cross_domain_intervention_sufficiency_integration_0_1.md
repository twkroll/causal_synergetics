## 3. Strongest positive evidence

### 3.1 A common mathematical mechanism survives across domains

The frozen CORE boundary says unforced slaving or passive reduction does not guarantee intervention-relative sufficiency because interventions can excite hidden/slaved modes or destroy controlled invariance. The power-grid benchmark instantiates this mechanism directly: the synchronous manifold is exact and locally attracting for `u=0`, while a localized machine-2 step gives `(e_delta_dot,e_omega_dot)=(0,u)` on that manifold and produces material finite-horizon error in the representative-machine macro.

The neural minimal benchmark is not the same ODE mechanism, but it supports the broader programme question operationally: states that are indistinguishable by current function can have different future responses to a declared learning intervention.

### 3.2 The cross-domain result includes a successful low-dimensional control

The power-grid mean/COI coordinate remains exactly closed under the same intervention, with numerical closure error `3.885780586188048e-14`. This is scientifically valuable because it prevents the programme from equating intervention insufficiency with universal failure of reduction. The correct question is which pre-declared macro is sufficient for which intervention family.

### 3.3 Anti-cherry-picking discipline has survived negative evidence

The programme preserved:

- the response-coordinate `WEAK` result versus equal-dimensional raw PCA;
- the nuisance-invariance specification/classification `FAIL`;
- the exact symmetry-aware Gram-PCA ceiling;
- the decision to park that direction rather than repair it.

The power-grid benchmark was then prospectively frozen before execution and returned PASS without topology, parameter, horizon, macro, amplitude, baseline or threshold search. This strengthens the credibility of the process even though it does not create novelty.

## 4. Strongest negative evidence and limitations

1. **Prior-art ceiling remains severe.** Generic intervention-conditioned state descriptions, controlled equivalence, controlled closure and compressed sufficient states are established topics.
2. **The neural evidence is still synthetic and short-horizon.** Linear and ReLU results are one-step demonstrations; historical reachability uses an artificial auxiliary preparation mechanism.
3. **The learned-coordinate direction did not clear its discriminator.** The first coordinate pilot was WEAK against raw PCA; the nuisance follow-up exposed a specification gap and an equally strong symmetry-aware raw-state quotient.
4. **Power-grid evidence is deliberately minimal.** It uses one identical two-machine system and one representative-machine macro. The exact mean/COI control shows that an appropriate two-dimensional aggregate can remain sufficient.
5. **No controlled state-preparation capability has yet been established as a new programme result.** Neural Historical Reachability is an existence result under a specially constructed auxiliary history, not a general preparation method, and the literature audit already places much of function-preserving preparation close to prior art.
6. **No manuscript-level novelty claim is justified.** A coherent research programme and a sequence of exact benchmarks are not by themselves a novelty result.
