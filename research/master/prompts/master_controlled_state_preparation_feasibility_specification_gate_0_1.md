## Purpose

Determine whether exactly one narrow controlled-state-preparation benchmark can be prospectively specified and frozen without reopening the parked neural response-coordinate direction, searching multiple candidates by effect size, or claiming novelty.

The intended capability question is:

> Can an admissible preparation action deliberately change hidden/internal state while preserving a pre-declared current observable, macro or function, so that a separately frozen later intervention response changes in a targeted and measurable way under a fixed preparation budget?

This is a MASTER feasibility/specification gate only. It must not execute a preparation experiment or inspect a new preparation effect.

## Mandatory feasibility and design requirements

Before any future execution, freeze exactly:

1. **one domain and one model/system**; no parallel neural/grid candidate execution;
2. **current observable/macro/function to preserve**, with exact or tolerance-based preservation criterion;
3. **hidden/internal state variables or preparation degrees of freedom**;
4. **one preparation action family**, its admissibility constraints, duration/steps, amplitude/budget and cost;
5. **one deterministic initial-state construction** and any physical/reachability constraints;
6. **one later evaluation intervention family** fixed independently of observed preparation outcomes;
7. **one target response property** and pre-declared direction/materiality criterion;
8. **response horizon(s)** and observed channels;
9. **no-preparation baseline**;
10. **at least one strong structurally or physically informed comparator** appropriate to the selected domain;
11. **primary metrics** for preservation error, preparation cost, response change/benefit and any safety/admissibility constraint;
12. **null/sanity controls** and, where applicable, symmetry/conservation checks;
13. **numerical/optimization method**, tolerances, resolution, seeds if stochasticity is unavoidable, and software stack;
14. **exhaustive, disjoint PASS/WEAK/NULL/FAIL criteria**;
15. **anti-retuning rule** prohibiting alternative preparation policies, budgets, targets, horizons, models or domains after effect inspection;
16. **outcome-specific claim ceiling**.

## Candidate-selection discipline

The gate may conceptually assess admissibility of the already frozen neural and power-grid contexts, but it must select exactly one candidate before any execution and justify that choice by scientific structure, measurability and prior-art separation rather than expected effect magnitude.

A neural candidate must not reopen learned response-coordinate/null/nuisance work. If neural preparation is selected, the gate must explicitly distinguish the new benchmark from the already frozen `Neural Historical Reachability 0.1`, which is only an artificial auxiliary-gradient existence construction.

A power-grid candidate must not alter or retune the frozen `Power-Grid Minimal Benchmark 0.1`. Any preparation benchmark must be a new prospectively specified task branching from the frozen result, not a reclassification or optimization of that benchmark.

## Required scientific distinction

A valid preparation benchmark must test more than mere reachability of a hidden state. It must couple:

- preservation of the declared present observable/macro/function;
- a bounded/admissible preparation intervention;
- a predeclared future evaluation intervention;
- a targeted response change or robustness objective;
- explicit comparison with doing nothing and a strong domain baseline.

If this cannot be separated clearly from standard function-preserving reparameterisation, ordinary feedback/preconditioning, model editing, or trivial initialization choice, freeze STOP/PARK.
