1. **Claim compliance:** no statement exceeds Package P or the C1–C5 LIT ceilings.
2. **Abstract/title compliance:** no priority, novelty, new-field, new-theory, generic causal-state, or generic preparation claim.
3. **Theory compliance:** projectability/closure equivalence and finite-horizon bounds are explicitly positioned as established/subsumed or standard.
4. **Neural compliance:** C5 remains illustrative/SAME-level; WEAK/FAIL/Gram/PARK evidence remains visible and numerically faithful.

6. **APP-C compliance:** preparation is framed as known-disturbance, model-based, output-constrained / preview-feedforward / preventive-control benchmark instantiation, not new/optimal/robust/generic control.
7. **Evidence fidelity:** numerical values and classifications match canonical frozen results; no cherry-picked omission or new metric.
8. **Negative-evidence visibility:** required claim-limit evidence is in main text, not hidden only in appendices.
9. **Citation compliance:** mandatory nearest-predecessor groups are cited in the required manuscript locations; identify only concrete missing metadata/placement issues.
10. **Architecture compliance:** all required sections and appendix roles are present; no unauthorised scientific section or new contribution list.
11. **Figure/table compliance:** only the frozen inventory and frozen metrics/analytic definitions are specified; no request for new simulations.
12. **Submission-readiness blockers:** distinguish scientific blockers from editorial/bibliographic/presentational blockers.
