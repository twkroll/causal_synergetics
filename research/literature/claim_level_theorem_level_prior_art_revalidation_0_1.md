## 1. Executive verdict

The targeted revalidation materially narrows the manuscript claim set.

The strongest negative result is C5: the neural claim that function-equivalent ReLU parameterisations can exhibit different gradient-based training responses is now **SAME** at the claim level. In particular, the 2026 ICML paper by Lebeurrier, Vayer and Gribonval states directly that properly rescaled ReLU weights can implement the same function while their training dynamics differ substantially. Earlier Path-SGD and parameter-symmetry work already established the relevant rescaling invariance problem. The frozen APP-A constructions remain useful as minimal reproducible witnesses, but they are not a contribution-level novelty claim.

C1 is also substantially prior art. The equivalence between exact controlled response preservation and projectability/quotient closure is structurally established in control-system quotient, bisimulation and abstraction literatures. Classical synergetic slaving and controlled/singularly perturbed fast-slow reduction are mature literatures as well. What survives is not a new theorem of controlled closure, but a **specific bridge and diagnostic use**: start from a pre-declared classical synergetic order-parameter/slaving map, ask whether that particular map satisfies the already-established controlled-projectability criterion for a frozen intervention family, and express finite-horizon failure in terms of fast relaxation, slaving defect and intervention leakage. That bridge is classified **CLOSE**, not novel.

C3 is **CLOSE** to long-standing coherency-based dynamic equivalencing. Coherent-generator aggregation, representative/equivalent generators, and center-of-inertia/aggregate coordinates are standard. The exact normalized two-machine witness is therefore an illustrative exact case, not a new power-system reduction principle.

C4 is **CLOSE** to geometric output-nulling reachability/zero dynamics, preview/feedforward control, trajectory shaping, and preventive power-system control. Steering hidden/internal state while preserving an output is a classical control concept; using knowledge of a future disturbance to prepare the system is also established. The frozen APP-C construction may be retained only as a particularly transparent two-stage benchmark that instantiates those ideas inside the same diagnostic fibre used by C3.

C2 is **RELATED** rather than a standalone contribution: the cross-domain pattern is consistent with known facts in controlled abstraction, neural optimization geometry and coherency reduction. Its value is evidential synthesis under prospectively frozen examples, not universality.

The package-level claim is classified **DISTINCT-ENOUGH-FOR-RESTRICTED-CLAIM** with action **RETAIN-RESTRICTED**. The reason is narrower than novelty of any ingredient: the programme applies one explicit diagnostic question to a *pre-existing synergetic macro* rather than proposing a new optimal abstraction, links that question to standard controlled projectability, preserves successful countercontrols (symmetry-aware neural quotient and exact mean/COI closure), and then demonstrates in the same physical fibre that output-preserving hidden-state preparation can change the later intervention response. The literature search found the ingredients separately and in close neighboring combinations, but did not identify an exact predecessor for this synergetics-centered theorem/diagnostic/evidence/preparation organization. Absence of an exact predecessor is **not** novelty proof; it is sufficient here only for restricted synthesis wording.

Accordingly, manuscript planning may proceed only under the allowed wording in Section 10 and mandatory citation positioning in Section 12.

## 4. Strongest SAME/CLOSE predecessors by claim

### C1

**Strongest structural SAME predecessor for projectability/quotient closure**

- P. Tabuada and G. J. Pappas, “Quotients of Fully Nonlinear Control Systems,” *SIAM Journal on Control and Optimization* 43(5), 1844–1866. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1137/S0363012901399027

The paper develops quotients of nonlinear control systems and explicitly introduces projectability, relating it to controlled invariance. This is strong prior art against any claim that controlled projectability/closure itself is new.

**Classical synergetic slaving**

- H. Haken, “Slaving principle revisited,” *Physica D* 97 (1996), 95–103. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/0167-2789(96)00080-2

This is the canonical predecessor for parametrizing high-dimensional state by order parameters near instability and deriving order-parameter equations.

**Approximate controlled equivalence / reduction error**

- A. Girard and G. J. Pappas, “Approximate bisimulation relations for constrained linear systems,” *Automatica* 43 (2007), 1307–1317. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.automatica.2007.01.019

This provides controlled-input approximation language and explicit precision/error concepts for reduced systems.

**Fast-slow controlled reduction / ISS**

- P. V. Kokotović, R. E. O’Malley Jr. and P. Sannuti, “Singular perturbations and order reduction in control theory — An overview,” *Automatica* 12 (1976), 123–132. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/0005-1098(76)90076-5
- P. D. Christofides and A. R. Teel, “Singular perturbations and input-to-state stability,” *IEEE Transactions on Automatic Control* 41(11) (1996), 1645–1650. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1109/9.544001

These are strong predecessors against novelty claims for bounds based on fast relaxation, disturbance/input leakage, stability of boundary layers, or standard comparison/ISS arguments.

### C2

There is no single SAME predecessor required to demote C2 because the component phenomenon is already distributed across the C1/C3/C5 literatures: state aggregation can be input-dependent, coherency is disturbance-dependent, and neural training dynamics can depend on parameter state even at fixed function. The cross-domain juxtaposition is therefore evidence organization rather than a new scientific law.

### C3

**Coherency-based representative/equivalent generator**

- G. J. Berg and A. Ghafurian, “Representation of coherency-based equivalents in transient stability studies,” *Electric Power Systems Research* 6(4) (1983), 235–241. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/0378-7796(83)90035-4

This paper explicitly constructs an equivalent generator for a coherent group and can incorporate the equation of one generator in that group, making it a particularly close predecessor to a representative-machine formulation.

**Coherency identification and aggregation context**

- V. Sankaranarayanan, M. Venugopal, S. Elangovan and N. Dharma Rao, “Coherency identification and equivalents for transient stability studies,” *Electric Power Systems Research* 6(1) (1983), 51–60. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/0378-7796(83)90031-7
- S. Kai et al., “A review of power system dynamic equivalents for transient stability studies,” *The Journal of Engineering* 2022, 761–772. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1049/tje2.12157

The review confirms that coherency identification, generator aggregation and network reduction are standard dynamic-equivalencing procedures, including slow-coherency methods and aggregate machines.

The frozen APP-B example is therefore defensible only as an exact minimal witness of a known failure mode: a representative/coherent surrogate is exact on its declared coherent passive set but loses accuracy when a localized disturbance drives relative motion, while an aggregate mean/COI variable remains closed in the symmetric two-machine system.

### C4

**Output-nulling reachability / hidden-state motion with output constrained**

Geometric control theory has long formalized states reachable while the regulated output remains identically zero. A modern summary of the classical notion states that the output-nulling reachability subspace is the set of states reachable from the origin by a suitable control while simultaneously maintaining the output at zero. Representative source:

- L. Ntogramatzidis et al., geometric-control literature on output-nulling reachability; see the established definition summarized in “A general approach to the eigenstructure assignment for reachability and stabilizability subspaces,” *Systems & Control Letters* (2017), and related geometric-control references.
- A. Isidori, “The zero dynamics of a nonlinear system: From the origin to the latest progresses of a long successful story,” *European Journal of Control* 19(5) (2013), 369–378. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ejcon.2013.05.014

This is a strong conceptual predecessor to steering internal/hidden state while a macro/output is held fixed.

**Preview/feedforward for known future disturbance**

- G. C. Goodwin, M. M. Seron and J. A. De Doná, “Feedforward model predictive control,” *Annual Reviews in Control* 35(2) (2011), 199–206. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.arcontrol.2011.10.007

The paper explicitly defines preview as knowledge of future reference/disturbance values and notes that preview lets a controller prepare for future changes.

**Preventive/coherency-based power-system control**

- K. Verma and K. R. Niazi, “A coherency based generator rescheduling for preventive control of transient stability in power systems,” *International Journal of Electrical Power & Energy Systems* 45(1) (2013), 10–18. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ijepes.2012.08.072
- “Preventive Control for Dynamic Security of Power Systems,” *IFAC Proceedings Volumes* 28(26) (1995), 379–383. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/S1474-6670(17)44787-2

These are strong predecessors for changing the pre-contingency condition to improve the response to a known/assessed contingency.

Together these literatures prevent APP-C from being called a new preparation/control concept. Its retained value is that the exact two-machine construction is aligned with the same macro fibre used in APP-B, preserves that macro during preparation, uses bounded open-loop inverse dynamics, turns preparation off before evaluation, and includes no-preparation and sign-mismatched equal-cost comparators.

### C5

**Direct SAME-level current source**

- A. Lebeurrier, T. Vayer and R. Gribonval, “Path-conditioned training: a principled way to rescale ReLU neural networks,” ICML 2026 / PMLR 306; arXiv:2602.19799. https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.19799

The paper states directly that two properly rescaled ReLU weight configurations can implement the same function while their training dynamics differ dramatically. This is essentially the frozen C5 proposition at the qualitative claim level.

**Earlier symmetry/optimization predecessors**

- B. Neyshabur, R. Salakhutdinov and N. Srebro, “Path-SGD: Path-Normalized Optimization in Deep Neural Networks,” NeurIPS 2015. https://papers.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2015/hash/eaa32c96f620053cf442ad32258076b9-Abstract.html
- L. Dinh, R. Pascanu, S. Bengio and Y. Bengio, “Sharp Minima Can Generalize For Deep Nets,” ICML 2017, PMLR 70:1019–1028. https://proceedings.mlr.press/v70/dinh17b.html

Path-SGD explicitly targets a geometry invariant to output-preserving weight rescaling. Dinh et al. use ReLU parameter symmetries to construct functionally equivalent networks with strongly altered parameter-space geometry. These already make Euclidean-gradient behavior under reparameterization a well-known issue.

Therefore C5 must be **DEMOTED** to a minimal pedagogical/benchmark witness. The historical auxiliary-gradient construction may be reported as provenance of the frozen states if useful, but it is not a manuscript contribution claim and it does not rescue C5.

## 5. Theorem-to-theorem analysis for C1

### 5.1 Exact fibre response homogeneity iff controlled projectability

For the frozen response functional — the complete retained `q(t)` trajectory over a finite horizon and an intervention family containing constant controls — the CORE proof shows:

`same q + identical q-trajectories for every admissible intervention`

iff

`f(q,r,u)` is constant over each `q`-fibre

iff

there is a closed projected dynamics `q_dot = f_bar(q,u)`.

**Audit judgment:** the mathematical content is **SAME at the structural level** to nonlinear controlled quotient/projectability and exact abstraction/lumpability ideas. Tabuada–Pappas is a direct control-system predecessor. The manuscript may use the equivalence, but may not advertise it as a new theorem of state abstraction, causal equivalence, or controlled closure.

### 5.2 Classical unforced slaving does not imply controlled projectability

Haken-style slaving constrains dynamics on/near an unforced invariant or attracting low-dimensional relation. Controlled quotient projectability requires a stronger condition across all states in a declared fibre and across admissible controls. The CORE counterexample makes this logical distinction explicit with one retained, one fast, and one control coordinate.

**Audit judgment:** **CLOSE**. The exact phrasing as a bridge from a pre-existing synergetic slaving map to a controlled quotient test is sufficiently specific to retain as the manuscript’s organizing mathematical observation, but the underlying ingredients and the fact that forcing can invalidate unforced reduction are not new.

Allowed role: a **translation/boundary theorem** that tells a synergetics reader exactly which extra standard control-theoretic property is required before an order parameter can be treated as intervention-sufficient.

Prohibited role: a “new controlled state equivalence theorem” or “first proof that slaving fails under forcing.”

### 5.3 Finite-horizon diagnostic/bound

The exact scalar counterexample and the more general contraction/ISS-style estimate express response error through relaxation rate, initial/slaving defect, and intervention leakage into fast variables.

**Audit judgment:** **CLOSE / standard machinery**. Singular perturbation, ISS and approximate-bisimulation literatures already connect fast stability, inputs/disturbances and reduced-model error. The programme-specific value is diagnostic packaging in terms of quantities associated with an already selected synergetic order parameter; the proof technique itself is not a novelty basis.

### 5.4 C1 retained boundary

C1 may be retained only as:

1. a precise cross-language bridge between classical synergetic slaving and standard controlled projectability;
2. an explicit warning that passive/unforced slaving does not certify intervention sufficiency;
3. a finite-horizon diagnostic packaging of standard fast-slow/error ingredients for a pre-declared macro.

It may not be retained as a new abstraction framework.

## 6. Domain-specific analysis for C3

The power-system literature already treats coherent generators as groups with similar disturbance trajectories and replaces groups by aggregate/equivalent generators for transient-stability studies. Representative-machine forms and inertial/COI-type aggregates are standard tools. Coherency itself is disturbance- and operating-condition-dependent, and modern reviews explicitly discuss accuracy limitations and refinements of coherent equivalents.

The frozen two-machine system nevertheless provides a clean controlled counterexample because it contains, in one analytically tractable model, all of the following:

- exact passive invariance of the coherent set;
- exact passive correctness of the pre-declared representative-machine macro on that set;
- immediate controlled-invariance failure under a localized hidden-machine step;
- a material representative-macro trajectory mismatch under the frozen metric;
- a simultaneous exact two-dimensional mean/COI closure control.

Those features make it a strong **diagnostic witness**, but they do not establish a new power-grid reduction theorem. In particular, the exact mean/COI control is scientifically important because it prevents the manuscript from implying that low-dimensional aggregation generically fails.

**C3 classification:** `CLOSE / RETAIN-RESTRICTED`.

## 7. Preparation/control analysis for C4

C4 combines three ideas with deep prior art:

1. **internal-state steering under output constraints** — geometric output-nulling reachability and zero dynamics;
2. **use of known future disturbance information** — preview and feedforward control;
3. **pre-contingency state modification** — preventive power-system control and coherency-based rescheduling.

Therefore “controlled state preparation” cannot be introduced as a new generic control primitive.

The frozen benchmark still has a useful restricted role because its construction is unusually explicit relative to the programme’s diagnostic fibre:

- the preserved quantity is exactly the same representative macro that was insufficient in APP-B;
- the moved coordinate is exactly the hidden coherency state exposed by the later localized intervention;
- the target is the known forced relative equilibrium for the later disturbance;
- the preparation is bounded and finite-duration;
- preparation is switched off before the evaluation phase;
- the matched preparation is compared against both no preparation and an equal-cost sign-mismatched preparation.

This makes C4 a good **within-programme constructive demonstration** that states on the same present macro fibre can be intentionally repositioned to alter a later intervention response. It does not make the control idea new.

**C4 classification:** `CLOSE / REINTERPRET`.

## 8. Neural symmetry and training-dynamics analysis for C5

The linear factorised example and the two-unit ReLU example are mathematically correct frozen witnesses. However, C5 is directly subsumed at the qualitative claim level.

For ReLU networks, positive-homogeneity rescalings preserve the represented function. Path-SGD was motivated by the fact that ordinary parameter-space optimization is not invariant to such output-preserving rescalings. Dinh et al. demonstrated that functionally equivalent ReLU models can have dramatically different parameter-space geometry. Most decisively, Lebeurrier, Vayer and Gribonval (ICML 2026) explicitly state that two properly rescaled parameter configurations can implement the same function while their training dynamics can be dramatically different and build a conditioning method around that fact.

The frozen APP-A one-step loss crossing is therefore best used as a minimal illustration of an established phenomenon that makes the programme’s intervention-response language concrete. The historical reachability construction can establish that the chosen linear endpoints are not merely disconnected algebraic points under the project’s artificial auxiliary protocol, but this does not change the prior-art classification.

The parked learned response-coordinate direction remains parked. The nuisance-invariance FAIL and symmetry-aware Gram control may be mentioned only if needed to document why the manuscript does **not** claim a uniquely useful learned causal/plasticity representation.

**C5 classification:** `SAME / DEMOTE`.

## 9. Package-level synthesis analysis

### 9.1 What would be merely a relabeling

The following package would be insufficient:

> “Controlled equivalence + slaving + neural symmetry + power-grid coherency + feedforward control, renamed causal synergetics.”

Every ingredient in that sentence has strong prior art. A conjunction and new terminology do not create a contribution.

### 9.2 Restricted synthesis that survives

A defensible package is narrower:

1. **Start from a pre-declared synergetic order parameter/slaving map**, not a learned or post-hoc optimized state abstraction.
2. **Translate intervention sufficiency into the standard controlled-projectability test**, explicitly acknowledging that the quotient criterion is prior art.
3. **Use the mismatch as a diagnostic of the chosen synergetic reduction**, with finite-horizon error expressed using standard fast-relaxation/leakage ingredients.
4. **Execute the same conceptual diagnostic prospectively in two distinct frozen minimal domains**, while retaining negative/limiting controls rather than suppressing them.
5. **In the physical domain, retain an exact successful alternative macro (mean/COI)** to show that the diagnosis is coordinate-specific rather than anti-reduction.
6. **Use the identified same-macro hidden direction constructively**: move within the present-macro fibre under bounded control, switch preparation off, and test the later response against matched/no/mismatched conditions.

The literature search located close predecessors for every component but did not locate this exact synergetics-centered sequence as a standard named framework or standard experimental package. That finding does **not** prove novelty. It does provide a defensible basis for a restricted synthesis manuscript whose contribution is the explicit connection, diagnostic organization, and prospectively frozen demonstration chain rather than ownership of the component concepts.

### 9.3 Package classification

**Primary class:** `DISTINCT-ENOUGH-FOR-RESTRICTED-CLAIM`

**Programme action:** `RETAIN-RESTRICTED`

The phrase “distinct enough” is used only in the governance-defined manuscript sense. It is not a novelty certification.
