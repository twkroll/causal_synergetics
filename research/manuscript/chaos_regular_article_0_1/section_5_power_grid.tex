\section{Power-Grid Minimal Witness}

\subsection{Symmetric two-machine swing model and coherent set}

The frozen power-system benchmark uses two identical classical swing machines with normalized $M=D=K=1$ and a lossless tie line:
\begin{align}
\dot\delta_1&=\omega_1,\\
\dot\omega_1&=-\omega_1+\sin(\delta_2-\delta_1),\\
\dot\delta_2&=\omega_2,\\
\dot\omega_2&=-\omega_2+\sin(\delta_1-\delta_2)+u(t).
\end{align}
The pre-declared representative macro is $q=(\delta_1,\omega_1)$ and hidden coherency coordinates are $e_\delta=\delta_2-\delta_1$, $e_\omega=\omega_2-\omega_1$. Their exact transverse dynamics are
\begin{align}
\dot e_\delta&=e_\omega,\\
\dot e_\omega&=-e_\omega-2\sin(e_\delta)+u.
\end{align}
For $u=0$, $e_\delta=e_\omega=0$ is invariant. Its transverse Jacobian $\left(\begin{smallmatrix}0&1\\-2&-1\end{smallmatrix}\right)$ has eigenvalues $(-1\pm i\sqrt7)/2$, so the coherent set is locally exponentially attracting around synchrony. The benchmark does not infer a broader attraction statement.

The frozen interventions are $u=0,+0.2,-0.2$, the horizon is $T=5$, and initial coherent speeds are $-0.1,0,+0.1$ with zero angles. Simulation used deterministic float64 RK4 at primary $dt=0.001$ with audit $dt=0.0005$; the maximum primary/half-step discrepancy was $8.1601392309949\times10^{-15}$.

\subsection{Representative-machine passive exactness}

On the coherent passive set, the representative macro obeys exactly $\dot\delta_1=\omega_1$, $\dot\omega_1=-\omega_1$. The frozen passive-slaving representative model B0 is therefore exact for all three declared passive coherent initial states: $E_{\rm pass}=0$. This is precisely the setting in which a passive/current description appears maximally favorable: the chosen representative reduction is not merely approximate on the declared passive set; it is exact.

\subsection{Localized hidden-machine disturbance and representative mismatch}

Under a nonzero local input, the coherent set is not controlled invariant. On $e_\delta=e_\omega=0$, $(\dot e_\delta,\dot e_\omega)=(0,u)$. The hidden-machine step immediately drives relative motion, while B0 continues to enforce the passive substitution and contains no direct $u$ term. Across frozen initial states and both step signs, the minimum controlled B0 full-trajectory max error is $E_{B0,\min}=0.3549858420076152$. For the zero-speed initial condition and either sign, the representative-machine error is $0.354985842007615$, while the full-model coherency maxima are approximately $\max|e_\delta|=0.130695487686682$ and $\max|e_\omega|=0.0895420239369532$. The physical-admissibility condition remains inside the frozen $|e_\delta|<\pi/2$ bound. A coherent aggregate surrogate B1 is much closer than B0, with minimum controlled representative mismatch $E_{B1,\min}=0.06534774384333092$, but B1 is not exact for the representative-machine coordinate under the localized disturbance.

\subsection{Exact arithmetic mean/COI closure countercontrol}

For
\begin{equation}
\delta_{\rm mean}=\frac{\delta_1+\delta_2}{2},\qquad \omega_{\rm mean}=\frac{\omega_1+\omega_2}{2},
\end{equation}
the antisymmetric line-power terms cancel exactly:
\begin{align}
\dot\delta_{\rm mean}&=\omega_{\rm mean},\\
\dot\omega_{\rm mean}&=-\omega_{\rm mean}+u/2.
\end{align}
This is exactly the B1 aggregate dynamics. The frozen full-model versus mean/COI closure error is $3.885780586188048\times10^{-14}$ at numerical precision. The identities $\delta_1-\delta_{\rm mean}=-e_\delta/2$ and $\omega_1-\omega_{\rm mean}=-e_\omega/2$ explain why B1 is close to, but not exact for, the representative machine.

The mean/COI control is scientifically essential. The benchmark demonstrates that the pre-declared representative-machine coherent surrogate is intervention-insufficient under this localized step; it does not show that low-dimensional power-grid aggregation generally fails. This interpretation is consistent with established coherency/dynamic-equivalent literature.\cite{BergGhafurian1983,Sankaranarayanan1983,Kai2022}

\subsection{Interpretation within established coherency theory}

The grid witness mirrors the CORE diagnostic in a physical ODE. Passive coherence supplies an invariant and attracting relation, and a localized intervention violates controlled invariance of that relation. The representative coordinate then loses the exactness it had on the passive coherent set. At the same time, another two-dimensional aggregate is exactly closed under the same disturbance. The result is therefore best read as an exact coordinate-specific witness inside established coherency theory, not as a general verdict on coherent reduction.
