\section{Output-Preserving Preparation Benchmark}

\subsection{Same physical fibre and known later disturbance}

The preparation benchmark uses the same normalized two-machine model and representative macro $q=(\delta_1,\omega_1)$. The initial state is $(0,0,0,0)$. The later evaluation disturbance is a known local machine-2 step $a=+0.2$ or $a=-0.2$. For a constant later step, the hidden relative equilibrium satisfies $2\sin(e_\delta^*)=a$, so $e_\delta^*=\arcsin(a/2)$ and $e_\omega^*=0$. For $|a|=0.2$, $|e_\delta^*|=\arcsin(0.1)=0.1001674211615598$.

The benchmark asks whether the system can be moved within the same present representative-macro fibre so that, after preparation has ended, the subsequent localized disturbance produces the coherent-aggregate response. This is framed as output-constrained hidden-state steering with preview of the known later disturbance.\cite{Isidori2013,NtogramatzidisPadula2017,Goodwin2011,VermaNiazi2013,LiBose1995}

\subsection{Frozen bounded quintic/inverse-dynamics preparation}

For preparation sign $b$, the desired relative path is
\begin{equation}
e_d(t;b)=e_\star(b)\left(10\xi^3-15\xi^4+6\xi^5\right),\qquad \xi=t/2,
\end{equation}
over $\tau_{\rm prep}=2$, with $e_\star(b)=\arcsin(b/2)$. Exact inverse dynamics defines
\begin{align}
p_1(t;b)&=-\sin(e_d(t;b)),\\
p_2(t;b)&=\ddot e_d(t;b)+\dot e_d(t;b)+\sin(e_d(t;b)).
\end{align}
The resulting trajectory holds the representative macro fixed to numerical precision while moving the hidden relative state to the target. The matched preparation has terminal hidden-target error $4.6079385647875116\times10^{-15}$ and macro-preservation error $P_q=3.580739551835791\times10^{-15}$. The frozen peak input is $0.20881049376163438\le0.35$ and preparation energy is $0.04006381839386479\le0.25$. Sign reversal produces identical cost and exact input sign symmetry in the frozen implementation. Preparation is switched off before evaluation; during evaluation the inputs are exactly $p_1=0$, $p_2=a$.

\subsection{P0/PT/PM comparator design}

Three conditions are frozen: P0 is no preparation; PT is matched preparation using the sign corresponding to the known later disturbance; PM is equal-cost sign-mismatched preparation. PM uses the same preparation magnitude and energy as PT but moves the hidden state toward the opposite forced relative equilibrium. This prevents the interpretation that any pre-motion of the hidden state would suffice. No additional policy, feedback controller, optimizer, or comparator was introduced.

\subsection{Frozen PASS metrics}

For both later disturbance signs, PT matches the coherent aggregate B1 to numerical precision: $E_{\rm target,max}=2.076727044536923\times10^{-15}$. By contrast, $E_{\rm no,min}=0.06534774384334105$ and $E_{\rm mismatch,min}=0.1307357122731585$. The corresponding improvement fractions are $B0_{\min}=0.9999999999999682$ and $BM_{\min}=0.9999999999999841$. The maximum absolute relative angle over all preparation/evaluation trajectories is $0.16130400338475567$, below the frozen coherent-regime safety threshold $\pi/2$. Primary/audit numerical discrepancy is $5.738465258531278\times10^{-15}$. P0 reproduces the APP-B $I_{\rm zero}$ coherent-aggregate mismatch within $5.551115123125783\times10^{-17}$, linking the preparation benchmark directly to the same physical witness rather than to a separately tuned system.

\subsection{Relation to output-nulling, preview/feedforward, and preventive control}

The preparation result should be interpreted narrowly. Geometric control already formalizes internal-state motion under output constraints, and zero dynamics provide a standard language for internal behavior compatible with constrained output.\cite{Isidori2013,NtogramatzidisPadula2017} Preview/feedforward methods use known future disturbance information to shape the current trajectory, while preventive power-system control changes the pre-contingency state in anticipation of a future event.\cite{Goodwin2011,VermaNiazi2013,LiBose1995}

The retained value here is alignment of those established ideas with the diagnostic fibre from Sec.~V: the output held fixed is exactly the representative macro that was insufficient; the hidden coordinate moved is exactly the coherency mode exposed by the localized disturbance; the target is the forced relative equilibrium; preparation is bounded and finite-duration; preparation is removed before evaluation; and the matched condition is compared with both no preparation and an equal-cost directionally wrong preparation.
